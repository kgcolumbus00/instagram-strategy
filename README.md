## Key Takeaways from Strategy Session

### The Audience Reality
- Most followers are real people met through school (K–college) — a warm, personal network, not a creator audience
- They follow because they know Kevin, not because they discovered him through content
- High story view rate expected relative to follower count — these are genuinely curious people
- Low-effort posting is riskier than with strangers — these people have an existing image of him

### The Content Identity (POV, Not Niche)
- **Don't be "travel guy" or "concert guy"** — one-dimensional niches are easy to tune out
- **Be the person with a point of view** — the *way* you see and capture things, not the subject
- Range is the personality: travel, concerts, random candid moments, quiet scenes — unified by taste and energy
- The DJI Osmo is the differentiator — cinematic quality at a concert or on a street in Japan immediately separates from 90% of the feed
- **Post less, not more** — scarcity and confidence create the "can't look away" effect
- Don't over-explain in captions — let the content speak

### What's Working
- Profile photo: clear, approachable, good shot
- 4 highlights (JP, CLG, HN, MX) hint at an interesting, well-traveled life without oversharing — exactly the right "mystique"
- 713 real followers with 1 post = coiled spring, audience waiting to be fed

### What Needs Work
- **1 post** — anyone landing on the profile has nothing to stay for; need 6-9 posts before doing anything else
- **933 following vs 713 followers** — ratio signals follower-chasing; clean up intentionally (not mass unfollow — these are real people)
- **Bio** — "Ohio State Alum" undersells; one extra line hinting at personality/interests/travel has room

## Content Strategy Framework

### The Formula
**Cinematic quality + authentic moments + intentional scarcity = captivating, not overwhelming**

### What to Post
- Travel footage (JP, MX, wherever's next) — especially DJI Osmo clips with a solid color grade
- Concert moments — crowd energy, artist shots, behind-the-scenes security POV if interesting
- Candid life moments that show range — not staged, not over-produced
- Occasional funny or relatable content that fits the "fun, chill, smart" identity people already know

### What to Avoid
- Posting just to stay "consistent" when the content doesn't represent you
- Chasing trends that don't fit the vibe
- Over-captioning — if you need three sentences to explain the post, the post isn't working
- Making travel the *entire* personality — it's one dimension of many

### The Multi-Platform Workflow (When Ready)
Film once → edit for vertical 9:16 first (Reels/TikTok) → export clean version → adapt for YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels → one shoot, 4–5 platforms
