Instagram Follower Tracker

See exactly who doesn't follow you back — fully private, runs in your browser only.

How to get your Instagram data

  1. Open Instagram → tap your profile → ☰ menu → Settings and privacy
  2. Scroll to Your activity → Download your information
  3. Tap Download or transfer information → Some of your information
  4. Select Followers and following only, then tap Next
  5. Choose Download to device → set format to JSON → request download
  6. Check your email in a few hours — Instagram will send a zip file
  7. Unzip it and find the followers_and_following folder
  8. Upload followers_1.json and following.json below

Your data never leaves your browser. No server, no account needed.
